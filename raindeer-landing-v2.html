The mesh

The mark is the architecture.

Our antlers were always a node graph. Ten specialized agents branch upward, an orchestrator sits at the head, and three outputs complete the muzzle. Hover any agent to see what it talks to.

Activation order mirrors the live pipeline — it plays once, then holds.

Under the hood

Boring, auditable machinery.

The interesting part of an agent system isn't the agents. It's what stops them going wrong.

Orchestration

CrewAI crews, LangGraph control flow

Role-based crews handle generation; a state graph handles the branching, retries and hand-offs. Every run is a replayable trace, not a black box — you can open any post and see which agent wrote which line, and why.

Memory

Brand Brain — retrieval, not vibes

Your posts, offers, docs and voice profile are embedded and retrieved per draft. Change your positioning once and every downstream agent inherits it on the next run. 412 vectors and counting.

Scheduling

Thompson sampling, not a static calendar

The Scheduler treats posting windows as a multi-armed bandit: it explores unproven slots at a controlled rate, then concentrates on what your audience actually shows up for.

Control

Human-in-the-loop, by default

Nothing publishes without an approval. Every agent action is logged, diffable and reversible — and you can pin any agent to manual at any time without stopping the rest of the mesh.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Does it post without asking me?

No. Human-in-the-loop is the default and can't be silently disabled. Every post lands in an approval queue; you can approve individually, approve a whole week, or pin any agent to manual. Nothing reaches a platform until you say so.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT for posts?

A chat gives you one draft from one model with no memory of your business. Raindeer runs ten agents against your Brand Brain — your past posts, offers and voice — then argues internally before anything reaches you. It also researches, designs, schedules and measures, which a chat window structurally cannot do.

What actually happens in those 28 seconds?

Trend Intel and Research pull and rank signals; Brand Brain retrieves your context; the Strategist picks angles; the Writer Crew drafts in parallel; the Editor cuts; the Visual agent renders; the Scheduler places each post in a learned peak window. The Orchestrator coordinates the hand-offs and writes the trace.

Which platforms are live?

LinkedIn is live and deepest. X, Instagram and Threads are in the waitlist rollout. We ship deep before we ship wide — a shallow integration on five platforms is worse than a real one on one.

Who owns the content and the data?

You do, entirely. Your Brand Brain is yours; export it or delete it at any time. We don't train shared models on your content.
